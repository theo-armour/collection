* Heat death of the universe is trillions of years away.

## Mission/ Deliverables

### Entirely Digital Archive

* **Everything** is scanned or photographed or otherwise digitized
* Hosted on GitHub.
* Hosted on archive.org
* Other online hosting platforms as decided
* no hosting, domain name, or other fees
* Free and open access for anyone interested
* Preserved for long-term accessibility and historical record
* Easily searchable and organized for future reference
* Redundant copies to prevent data loss
* Regularly updated to include new materials
* Metadata and context included for each item
* High-quality images and scans for all items
* Open formats and standards used for long-term compatibility
* Clear documentation and instructions for accessing and using the archive
* Compliance with legal and ethical standards for digital archiving
* Community involvement and collaboration encouraged
* Regular review and assessment of the archive's effectiveness and relevance
* Long-term sustainability and maintenance plan in place
* Clear succession plan for future caretakers of the archive
* Regular backups and disaster recovery plan in the hands of the hosting services and archive maintainers


### Physical Objects

All I'm looking for personally are the images, scans, or digitized formats of the things.

My future is about making these things come alive, giving them an aura, and adding value to them.

For most of the actual physical objects themselves, I could care less. Obviously, things that are museum quality should find new homes. Ditto, items that are collectible and collectors would like. The rest can be disposed of responsibly, recycled, or otherwise managed without sentimentality.

A few of the items I might wish to keep myself until I die
A few of the items may be picked up by my children or another of the relatives.
Otherwise, it's finding new homes for old things.

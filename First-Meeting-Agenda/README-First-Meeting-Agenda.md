# First Meeting Agenda

## Introductions

* May I record this meeting?
* Can I publish our correspondence?
* Why Archive?
* What skills and expertise can you bring to the project?
* Do have easy access to a car?
* What are your goals for this project?
* What would you like to learn?

## Terminology / Policies / Procedures / Templates

* Ascertaining what I know and don't know about archiving
* What should I read? What websites should I look at? Any recommended articles or books?
* Ways of documenting the process and progress of the project
* Specific policies or procedures to follow during the project?
* Templates to use for documentation or reporting?
* Guidelines or best practices to follow during the project?

## Collection Surveying: template?

* Proposed methodology for collection surveying
* Tools and resources needed for collection surveying
* What would an ideal overview of collection look like?
* Expected outcomes and deliverables from collection surveying

## Collection Management

* Strategies for organizing and cataloging the collection
* Handling and storage requirements for different types of items
* Preservation and conservation considerations
* Access and usage policies for the collection
* Documentation and record-keeping for collection management

## Scheduling / Timing / Program management

* Setting timelines and responsibilities
* Discussion of digitization plans
* Forever project

## Pricing / Compensation

* Discussing compensation structure: Hourly vs Fixed rate
* Collaboration
* If I split up the duties, which duties would you like?
* I would be happy to work with more than one.

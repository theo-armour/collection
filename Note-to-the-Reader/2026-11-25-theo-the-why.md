## Prompt 1

Theo is sitting at his desk, looking at his computers with a dazed expression and shaking his head. Natalis de Wailly Enters the picture. and puts his hand on Theo's shoulder. Theo is startled. Natalie, in his 19th-century French-English accent  says, "Oh, I startled you. I'm sorry."

I saw you looking Confused and thought maybe I could help. My name is Natalis de Wailly. You could say I wrote the textbook on archiving. People often ask me, "Why do I do this?"

I'm reminded of a quote by the famous Sir Edmund Hillary. Sir Edmund lived long after my death, but I know about him well because his life is well archived.

People often asked Sir Edmund, "Why did you climb Mount Everest?" He was said to have replied "Because it is there". It was not really his quote, but let us not ruin a good story for the sake of the argument.

So nowadays, when people ask me, "Why do you archive?" I reply "because it is there. Then I pause for a few seconds and say, "And, well, duh, If there was nothing there, there'd be nothing to archive."

As he says this, Natalie throws his hands up in the air and giggles in a wonderful French way.

And we both laugh

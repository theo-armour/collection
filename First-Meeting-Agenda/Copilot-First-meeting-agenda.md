# CopilotFirst Meeting Agenda

## Purpose

Learn about each other's experience and expectations, introduce the collection and its goals, and agree on a sensible next step. If several applicants attend together, use this as an informational meeting and save individual evaluation and sensitive details for follow-up conversations.

**Suggested length:** 75 minutes

## Agenda

1. **Welcome and introductions — 10 minutes**
   * Introductions, relevant experience, and what each person hopes to learn.
   * Explain the purpose and format of the meeting.

2. **Collection overview — 10 minutes**
   * What the collection contains, where it is held, and its history and provenance.
   * Known gaps in documentation, condition, ownership, or rights.
   * What is already known about archiving and what needs to be learned.

3. **Goals and priorities — 10 minutes**
   * Preserve historical context and follow appropriate archival standards.
   * Discuss digitization, including 3D scanning, and how to prioritize it.
   * Consider whether some items might be better placed with another home or institution.
   * Treat this as a long-term project; establish priorities rather than assume everything can be digitized at once.

4. **Applicants' approach — 20 minutes**
   * How would you start surveying and documenting the collection?
   * What information would you need before proposing a plan?
   * How do you balance preservation, access, privacy, rights, and cost?
   * What would you recommend doing first, and what could wait?
   * Which specialists or institutions might be useful partners?

5. **Working together and practicalities — 15 minutes**
   * Discuss pace, communication, decision-making, and how to record recommendations.
   * Make room to develop ideas, then invite candid questions and professional concerns.
   * Discuss likely time commitments, scope, pricing or compensation, and a sustainable payment schedule.
   * Explore long-term collaboration and networking without assuming commitments.

6. **Questions and next steps — 10 minutes**
   * Identify unanswered questions and information each side will provide.
   * Agree on follow-up materials, an initial survey, or individual interviews.
   * Set a follow-up date and explain how applicants will hear about next steps.

## Before the Meeting

* Share a short overview of the collection and the goals above.
* Only share materials appropriate for all attendees.
* Ask separately for permission before publishing correspondence, notes, or comments; consent is optional and unrelated to selection.
* Ask whether attendees want to receive future project updates.

## Desired Outcomes

* A sense of each applicant's experience and working approach.
* A list of information needed to survey the collection.
* An outline for a possible first phase, including scope, timing, and compensation.
* Clear follow-up steps and communication preferences.

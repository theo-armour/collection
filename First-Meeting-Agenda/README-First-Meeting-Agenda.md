# First Meeting Agenda

Update 2026-11-26

The headings are the things we should cover. The bullets are just suggestions for aspects that might be discussed. I assume we could spend about an hour plus on this first effort.

There are two other files in this folder. They are copies of how different AI apps responded to what a "What should be in a first meeting agenda" prompt.

To be read in conjunction with:

* https://theo-armour.github.io/collection/#Note-to-the-Reader/README-Notes-to-Reader.md

## Introductions

* May I record this meeting?
* May I publish our correspondence?
* What skills and expertise can you bring to the project?
* Do you have easy access to a car?
* What are your goals for this project?
* What would you like to learn?
* Why archive?
* What is your archival dream?
* Future engagement possibilities for the Julia Morgan Building and the San Francisco Ladies Protection Relief Society
* GitHub user name?

## Terminology / Policies / Procedures

* Ascertaining what I know and don't know about archiving
* What should I read? What websites should I look at? Any recommended articles or books?
* Ways of documenting the process and progress of the project
* Specific policies or procedures to follow during the project?
* Guidelines or best practices to follow during the project?
* Understanding and applying EAD and DACS in practice
* Using AI in the archival process

## Collection Surveying

* Proposed methodology for collection surveying
* Tools and resources needed for collection surveying
* What would an ideal overview of collection look like?
* Expected outcomes and deliverables from collection surveying
* Templates to use for documentation or reporting?

## Collection Management

* How would you approach organizing this collection, phase 2?
* Strategies for organizing and cataloging the collection
* Handling and storage requirements for different types of items
* Preservation and conservation considerations
* Access and usage policies for the collection
* Documentation and record-keeping for collection management

## Scheduling / Timing / Program management

* Setting timelines and responsibilities
* Discussion of digitization plans
* Forever project: This has no end date. How do you work with open-ended clients?

## Pricing / Compensation

* Discussing compensation structure: Hourly vs Fixed rate
* Do you have collaborators for carrying out specific tasks ?
* If I split up the duties, which duties would you like?
* I would be happy to work with more than one.

## Next steps and follow-up meeting

* Links to Efforts you have worked on previously?
* What might you need?
* No specific deliverable after this meeting. But feel free to send any comments or good thoughts.
* The real deliverable will be after your visit to storage. We will review the findings and plan the next steps accordingly.
* When should we schedule the visit to storage?

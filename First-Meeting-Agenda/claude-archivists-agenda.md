# Archivists Agenda

## 2026-11-25 First meeting agenda (~60 min)

For the first meeting with each archivist who replied to the 2026-11-17 RFP. A get-to-know-you meeting, before any paid consultation or storage-unit visit.

### 1. Introductions (10 min)

* Who I am and why I'm doing this ("I like history… I want to be involved in making history")
* Their background, and why the RFP caught their eye

### 2. The collection in brief (10 min)

* 200+ linear ft in three storage units, hard drives, ~500 GB across cloud services
* Family threads: Norman Armour, George Armour, Burrage, Lowrie, the Koudacheffs, plus my AutoCAD-era working archive
* Bring 3–5 sample items or photos of the units

### 3. My goals, and what they think of them (15 min)

The most revealing part.

* Public, open-licensed digital access (CC BY 4.0) at Internet Archive, Permanent.org, GitHub. Where do they think it belongs?
* **Recycling most originals**, offering "keepers" to Princeton, Art Institute, etc. Raise early: some archivists will push back, and their reaction tells me a lot.
* I fund the processing; institutions carry it long term

### 4. Their approach (10 min)

* How they'd run the paid assessment: what they'd look at, how long the visit takes
* What the written assessment would contain: phased plan, cost estimate, format
* Views on metadata (Dublin Core/METS), formats (PDF/A), scanning services vs. in-house
* How they handle sensitive or private family material

### 5. Practicalities (10 min)

* Proposed rate for the consultation; typical rates for later phases
* Availability: first visit within 60 days?
* Would they do later phases themselves, supervise others, or advise only?
* One or two references, or a sample of a past personal-collection project

### 6. Next steps (5 min)

* When their consultation proposal arrives
* What they need from me before a visit: access, inventory, links

### Tips

* Ask all three the same core questions (sections 3–5) so I can compare them fairly
* Send a short pre-read: the collection link and a one-page version of the long-term outcome doc
* Listen for "take charge" energy: my tasks list (scanning logistics, verifying copies before destruction, finding new homes) needs someone who drives

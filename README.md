## Purpose of this Repository

* Document and share the process of creating a template and enabling of online collections
* Serve as a central hub for managing, maintaining, and sharing the collection
* Act as a repository from which the material can be accessed and utilized
* Act as a catalog for links to the collected items in other locations
* Provide a structured and searchable curation of the collection

## Armour _et al_ Genealogy

See: https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/

Very much a work in progress.

* Documents family history and lineage.
* Maintains records of family members and their relationships.
* Provides a structured and searchable archive of genealogical information.
* Provides evidence of the possible notability of relevant family members for drafting future articles for Wikipedia

On Wikipedia, notability is a test used by editors to decide whether a given topic warrants its own article.

See: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Notability

In my book, everybody is notable. If there is a right to be forgotten, then there also must be a right to be remembered. Even so, I kind of enjoy looking up the ones that had fun, engaging, and perhaps therefore more notable lives.

I have put some of them in a list here that I call "Peeps in My Family".

See: https://theo-armour.github.io/genealogy/#Peeps-in-My-Family.md

Much of the information stored in the bank boxes in my storage locker is about these people. These papers are the subject of the current request. Linking the primary and secondary source items in storage to these people is the first step in establishing a comprehensive archival record.

## Personal Items

A significant part of the effort will be getting high-quality images of all of the following items: art collection, furniture, and personal items.

For just about every item in each of these collections here, I have a story to tell. A picture creates a thousand memories, or something. In other words, a picture is just as good as seeing the object itself for many of the purposes that I plan to use these images for.

Of course, the picture does not have the "aura" as described by Walter Benjamin. The unique presence and authenticity of the original object cannot be fully captured in an image.

Nonetheless, by giving my story about the object and then making the physical item available to collectors of the future, I can provide a richer context and preserve the legacy of the source items for posterity. And so, dealing with these items themselves is a possible later phase.

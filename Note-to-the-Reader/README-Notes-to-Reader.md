# Notes to the Reader

AI> These notes are intended to provide context and guidance for readers navigating the collection. They may include insights, explanations, and reflections on the materials presented.

## Folders

* [Artificial-Intelligence](Artificial-Intelligence/README-Artificial-Intelligence.md) — How I use AI and how it may change the making of a personal archive. Includes answers from Claude, Gemini and Copilot to the same question, with my writing kept separate from the AI's.
* [Genealogy](Genealogy/README-Genealogy.md) — Family history as the "scorecard" for the collection. Respect des fonds, the family tree as the way to group the sources, and notes on the genealogy web apps and their limits.
* [Goals](Goals/README-goals.md) — Mission, vision and values: the archive of a normal human life, "what is remembered lives", and legacy as the afterlife.
* [Journal](Journal/README-journal.md) — Dated entries, newest first. In other words, a blog: daily thinking on why we archive and how the work is going.
* [My-Core-Tools](My-Core-Tools/README-My-Core-Tools.md) — The tools, formats and methods I use nearly every day (GitHub, VS Code, Markdown, JSON, AI assistants), plus the collection's agenda and journal files. These are preferences, not requirements.
* [Reference](Reference/README-reference.md) — Background reading: archival concepts, standards, professional organizations, archiving terminology, and AI-written guides to archiving a family collection and creating a finding aid.
